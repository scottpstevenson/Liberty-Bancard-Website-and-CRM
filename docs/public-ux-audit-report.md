## Home (`/`)

**Scores:** Conversion Clarity 8/10 · Mobile Usability 7/10 · Information Hierarchy 6/10

**Findings:**

- The call-to-action buttons are somewhat buried in the content, which may reduce their visibility and effectiveness.
- The text in the hero section is quite dense and may overwhelm users; it could benefit from more whitespace or visual breaks.
- The mobile version lacks a clear visual hierarchy, making it difficult for users to quickly identify key actions and information.
- The statistics presented (e.g., potential savings) could be more prominently displayed to capture user interest immediately.

**Recommended fixes:**

- Increase the size and contrast of the call-to-action buttons and consider placing them above the fold for better visibility.
- Simplify the hero section text and add more whitespace to improve readability and user engagement.
- Implement a clearer visual hierarchy in the mobile layout, such as larger headings and more spacing between sections.
- Use larger, bolder fonts for key statistics to draw attention and consider using icons or visuals to represent savings.
