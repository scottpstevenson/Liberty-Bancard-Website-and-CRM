## Done looks like

No frozen-cohort prerequisite or manual named-email approval remains in ordinary supply; historical evidence remains intact.
- Selected current-email validation works before completed campaign/package/sequence binding. Exact email/hash/generation, relationship, selection, freshness and suppression remain checked at enqueue and transport; unselected CRM inventory does not become a bulk validation campaign. Fresh positive and negative receipts are reused; unknown/risky/unvalidated are not relabelled invalid.
- A rejected slot is retired/replenished from retained independent eligible alternatives without replacing every business commitment or duplicating fresh receipts. Paused preparation/membership automatically advances and stale qualification/suppression retires commitments, while pending validation is not send authority.
- Apollo 401 is durable auth/configuration unavailable and stops unchanged-revision retry loops; corrected supported configuration may resume without per-row approvals. OpenAI authentication/configuration, transport/rate-limit/timeout/refusal/truncation, invalid output and settlement errors preserve distinct safe references, known usage and uncertainty.

Unsupported outcome retrieval remains explicit uncertainty, never invented unspent credit or blanket retry.
- Overall completed ticks with failed linking/coverage/projection/preparation appear degraded with stage reason, retry/cooldown, freshness and last committed progress; fair local stages are not globally stopped by one failure. Equivalent-scope Contact, Business, Prospecting and Campaigns agree via existing shared readers.
- After separately approved production delivery, representative imported/registry/existing-CRM/provider-returned cases automatically reach current accepted/selected/validated or justified exceptions and paused memberships over sustained committed ordinary cycles and restarts. Real selected validation receipt/legitimate reuse and no unselected spend must be proved; open gates, heartbeats, source tests and publish alone do not close this acceptance.

### 7. Apollo is actively receiving authorization failures, not simply idle

the account lacks endpoint entitlement, or the deployment uses the wrong  
credential cannot be determined from this status alone. No secret was inspected  
and no external request was issued. Rotating keys without checking endpoint  
entitlement is not an established fix.

314 failed/committed operations, 211 failed/ambiguous operations, and 38  
running/reserved operations. Their precise provider/parser/settlement exceptions  
cannot be reconstructed from this collapsed label. The audit does not invent  
a single credential, token-parameter, or model failure to explain all of them.  
  
Reservation idempotency actually appends the owning run ID. A prompt hash is  
not a permanent cross-run failed-operation lock. The earlier hypothesis that  
the prompt-only key permanently blocks all new runs is disproved by the actual  
reservation implementation.
