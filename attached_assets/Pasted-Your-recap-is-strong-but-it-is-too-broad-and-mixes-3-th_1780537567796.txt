Liberty Bancard Replit App — Recommended Build Recap

The Liberty Bancard platform should be built as an AI-powered business operating system for merchant services. Its core job is to unify lead capture, CRM visibility, AI-assisted sales, merchant onboarding, residual tracking, partner referrals, growth analytics, and GHL automation into one command center.

Core system of record:

* Contacts
* Companies
* Deals
* Merchant applications
* Documents
* Tasks
* Tickets
* Partner organizations
* Agents
* Residual reports
* Merchant profiles
* Outreach state
* AI audit logs

External systems that should remain outside the app:

* GoHighLevel: email, SMS, workflows, calendars, voice AI, e-signature execution
* QuickBooks/Gusto: accounting, payroll, AP/AR, tax
* Google Drive: long-form file storage and SOP libraries
* Processor/gateway systems: MID data, transactions, chargebacks

What Replit should build first:

1. Master Command Dashboard
    A single executive dashboard showing leads, pipeline, booked calls, statements uploaded, proposals sent, closed won deals, onboarding status, residual revenue, partner referrals, SDR activity, and stuck items.
2. Universal Merchant Timeline
    Every contact/merchant should have one timeline combining forms, GHL messages, calls, notes, statement uploads, AI analysis, proposal history, onboarding steps, tickets, residual activity, and partner source.
3. GHL Sync Control Layer
    A reliable admin panel showing contact sync status, workflow enrollments, webhook logs, duplicate recovery, failed syncs, retry buttons, and field mappings.
4. Statement-to-Proposal Flow
    This should be the highest-value sales workflow:
    Lead submits statement → AI extracts fees → effective rate calculated → savings proposal generated → rep reviews → proposal sent → follow-up sequence triggered → deal stage updated.
5. Onboarding Command Center
    After Closed Won, the app should automatically create onboarding steps, equipment tasks, document checklist, underwriting/RFI tracker, MID status, and launch checklist.
6. Residual Revenue & Commission Module
    Build CSV upload, MID matching, variance detection, agent commission calculations, merchant profitability, and monthly reconciliation views.
7. Partner / ISO Portal
    Partners need referred leads, deal status, commission tracking, co-branded proposals, referral links, and marketing collateral.
8. Growth Attribution Dashboard
    Track every lead by source, campaign, UTM, landing page, partner, referral, and close rate. This should include UTM builder, QR generator, channel scorecard, and weekly signup targets.
9. AI SDR Operator Dashboard
    Show active leads, next scheduled touches, stuck leads, bounce risk, reply classification, channel health, compliance blocks, and global pause/resume controls.
10. Compliance & Audit Layer
    Every AI message, outbound attempt, sync event, stage change, and sensitive merchant update should be logged with actor, timestamp, source, and reason.

Biggest missing pieces:

* No clear canonical merchant ID connecting contact, deal, application, MID, residuals, tickets, and documents
* No single merchant timeline
* No end-to-end Closed Won → Onboarding → MID Live workflow
* No source-of-truth sync error dashboard for GHL
* No lifecycle reporting from first click to residual revenue
* No partner/referral attribution loop through closed revenue
* No clear AI approval workflow before sending sensitive pricing/compliance messages
* No equipment inventory or terminal serial tracking
* No internal project/task system outside deal-related tasks

Best architecture:
Replit should be the command center.
GHL should be the communication engine.
QuickBooks should be the financial record of truth.
Google Drive should be the document library.
Processor/gateway data should feed the app for merchant performance and residual reporting.

Build priority in Replit:

Phase 1: canonical merchant ID, merchant timeline, GHL sync logs, statement-to-proposal flow.
Phase 2: onboarding command center, residual/commission reconciliation, growth attribution dashboard.
Phase 3: partner portal, AI SDR operator dashboard, referral engine, equipment inventory.
Phase 4: internal project management, deeper reporting, advanced AI advisors.

Biggest recommendation: make Replit the single operating dashboard, not another CRM. Let GHL execute communications, but make Replit the place where you can see what is happening, what is stuck, what made money, and what needs action.
