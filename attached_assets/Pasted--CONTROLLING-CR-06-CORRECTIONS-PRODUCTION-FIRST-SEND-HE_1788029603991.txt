## CONTROLLING CR-06 CORRECTIONS — PRODUCTION-FIRST, SEND-HELD

### Operating objective

Deliver a functioning production cold-outreach preparation system now.

Discovery, imports, classification, enrichment, validation, readiness/scoring, campaign and sequence management, content approval, production cohort freezing, prepared enrollment, recipient rendering, held delivery intents, scheduling, queues, monitoring, and admin controls must operate.

Only the final external transport send remains closed by default during this task.

1. Do not replace `campaigns.status` or `follow_up_sequences.status` with the proposed eight-value list. Separate artifact kind, record class, purpose, governance state, compatibility disposition, preparation state, launch-gate state, and execution/dispatch state. Preserve unrelated transactional and operational sequences.

2. Use additive governance, version, and preparation tables or nullable opt-in links. Do not destructively rewrite existing production rows. Show existing campaigns and sequences in the production catalog as accurately classified legacy objects with review, clone, replace, pause, and retire controls.

3. CR-06 must deploy the governance system to production and run an explicit idempotent rollout that creates:

   * Three real production premium campaign programs
   * Three sequence versions
   * Twelve email content versions
   * Three post-Email-2 manual-task action definitions

   Do not use uncontrolled startup seeding or mutable marketing-copy migration DML.

4. The production rollout must support dry-run, exact manifest/hash/count verification, conflict detection, transactional or resumable ownership, audit evidence, and safe replay without duplicates. Objects begin as `draft` or `review_ready`; authorized admins may approve them as `approved_inactive`.

5. Campaign approval and recipient preparation are separate immutable artifacts. Approval freezes the content/variant, cadence, renderer, merge/evidence/fallback rules, compliance policy, sender policy, CR-04 compatibility, cap, quiet hours, stop rules, reviewer, expiry, and dependency versions. Production preparation later binds the exact cohort member, evidence, render hash, schedule, and idempotency key.

6. Add a governed production `prepare` command. It may:

   * Freeze the real CR-04 production cohort
   * Create prepared or held enrollments
   * Render exact recipient messages from current retained evidence
   * Create held delivery intents
   * Reserve the bounded pilot cap
   * Schedule the work
   * Place it in a durable `held_for_release` queue

   It must not invoke external email, SMS, voice, voicemail, or social transport.

7. Keep the unsafe legacy campaign-queue and human-enrollment endpoints denied. Do not leave production preparation disabled. Add governed prepare/hold routes and prove that only approved versions and frozen cohort members can enter them.

8. Preserve the authorized admin UI for global pause/unpause and campaign launch-gate control.

   * Closing a campaign gate or applying pause must always be immediate.
   * Opening requires the exact approved version and cohort, current preflight, cap, confirmation, expiry, idempotency, CSRF protection, and audit evidence.
   * Do not remove or permanently disable these admin controls.

9. Separate these four commands and authorities:

   1. Global pause/unpause
   2. Campaign launch-gate open/close
   3. Production prepare/hold
   4. Final dispatch/release

   Unpausing must not open a campaign gate or launch anything. Opening a campaign gate must not unpause or send. Preparing must not send. Dispatch must consume the exact approved held run and recheck every removal-only gate.

10. Replace or forward `/api/admin/outbound/cohort-launch`. Do not let it continue to lift the global pause and claim a launch in one mutation. Preserve admin control instead of permanently returning 403 or removing the launch controls.

13. Disposable certification must deny all external transports. That is a test-isolation rule—not a production feature-disable rule.

* Untrusted values are escaped.

* Preview, approval proof, held delivery intent, and eventual delivery must use the same renderer and versions.
