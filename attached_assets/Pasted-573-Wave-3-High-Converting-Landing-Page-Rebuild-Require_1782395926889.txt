573 — Wave 3: High-Converting Landing Page Rebuild

Required Skills

* frontend-design — premium conversion UX, responsive layout, CTA hierarchy, accessibility
* copywriting — merchant-facing offer copy, objection-aware messaging, high-intent landing page copy
* growth-marketing — CRO, paid/organic funnel alignment, CTA architecture, conversion path strategy
* fullstack-js — React/TypeScript routing, shared components, form/event handlers
* analytics-engineering — CTA/form/upload event instrumentation using existing analytics patterns
* compliance-systems — compliant surcharge/cash-discount/free-terminal language, no unsupported claims
* QA-release-lead — route smoke tests, mobile QA, regression validation

⸻

What & Why

Liberty Bancard’s high-intent public pages must convert more visitors into the four primary revenue actions:

1. Inbound phone calls
2. Booked appointments
3. Statement uploads / free analysis requests
4. Merchant application starts

The current website has the right page inventory, but the conversion system is not yet sharp enough:

* Homepage messaging is too general and needs a more specific merchant value proposition.
* Mobile users need a clear tap-to-call / book / upload path above the fold and/or sticky access.
* /beat-square-stripe is useful but too data-heavy and needs stronger conversion routing.
* /0-percent-processing / Liberty Zero™ language must be tightened so it does not overpromise “free processing.”
* /free-analysis and /upload-statement exist but need stronger trust proof, clearer next steps, and better mobile flow.
* /free-smart-terminal does not exist as a dedicated offer page; current CTA behavior routes users generically to /get-started.
* Competitor, industry, and location pages need consistent high-intent CTA architecture.
* CTA analytics must fire consistently for phone, booking, upload, and application-intent clicks.

This task rebuilds the high-intent conversion layer without changing core backend CRM/contactability systems.

⸻

Business Goal

Maximize qualified inbound calls, booked appointments, statement uploads, free analysis requests, and merchant application starts while keeping offer language compliant, accurate, and trustworthy.

⸻

Conversion Priority

Every high-intent page should support this CTA hierarchy:

1. Phone Call — fastest path for urgent/high-intent merchants
2. Book Appointment — best path for scheduled sales conversations
3. Upload Statement / Free Analysis — best proof-based conversion path
4. Start Application / Check Eligibility — only where the merchant has enough intent

Do not force every page to use all CTAs with equal visual weight. Each page should have one dominant primary CTA and 1–2 secondary actions based on intent.

⸻

Compliance + Claim Rules

Do not invent or exaggerate claims.

Trust proof rules

Only use trust indicators if they are verified from existing content, config, assets, or business-provided copy.

Do not invent:

* BBB accreditation
* PCI certification
* ISO registration/accreditation
* merchant count
* years in business
* partner logos
* “licensed agents”
* guaranteed approval
* guaranteed savings

If a trust signal cannot be verified, use neutral trust copy such as:

* “Secure statement upload”
* “No obligation review”
* “Reviewed by a Liberty Bancard specialist”
* “Transparent rate comparison”
* “Merchant-focused payment review”

Savings claim rules

Do not make unsupported savings claims.

Allowed:

* “See if Liberty can beat your current rate.”
* “Compare your current effective rate.”
* “Find out whether you may be overpaying.”
* “Upload a statement for a free savings review.”
* “Savings depend on your current processor, volume, card mix, business type, and pricing plan.”

Blocked unless backed by real calculator/statement data:

* “Save 30%”
* “Cut your fees in half”
* “Guaranteed savings”
* “Lowest rates guaranteed”
* “Free processing” without qualifier

Liberty Zero™ / fee-offset language rules

Do not use unqualified “free processing” as a standalone claim.

Preferred language:

* “Liberty Zero™ fee-offset program”
* “Explore compliant cash discount options”
* “Explore compliant surcharge review”
* “See whether your business qualifies for a compliant fee-offset setup”
* “Program availability depends on business type, state rules, card-network rules, disclosure requirements, and underwriting.”

Always include a clear disclaimer on /0-percent-processing above or near the first CTA.

Free smart terminal language rules

Do not imply guaranteed free equipment.

Preferred language:

* “Check if your business qualifies for a free smart terminal.”
* “Available for approved merchants.”
* “Terms apply.”
* “Eligibility may depend on processing volume, business type, underwriting, and equipment program requirements.”

Kill Lines

Stop and report before implementation if:

* Any required trust claim cannot be verified from existing business data/content/config.
* Any page currently says “free processing” as an unqualified standalone claim and the replacement language is unclear.
* Any proposed savings claim is not backed by calculator/statement data.
* Any CTA would route users into a broken or missing form.
* Booking URL source is unclear or missing.
* Phone number source is unclear or hardcoded inconsistently.
* Existing UTM capture would be broken by new CTA query params.
* Sticky mobile CTA overlaps existing form submit buttons, upload controls, cookie banner, or GHL chat widget.
* The /free-smart-terminal page requires backend eligibility logic that does not exist.
* Existing upload flow would be broken by progress/error state changes.
* Compare/industry/location templates are generated in a way that makes direct edits brittle.
* Adding the new route requires SEO/sitemap infrastructure changes outside this wave.
* The task requires enabling SMS, AI voice, or ringless voicemail.
* The task requires changing PEWC/contactability backend logic.
