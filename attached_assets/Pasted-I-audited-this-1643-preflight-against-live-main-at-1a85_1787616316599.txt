### Missing P0: health writes can destroy a successor’s lease

`background_jobs` currently serves as both:

* The logical execution lease.
* BullMQ worker-health telemetry.

The QueueManager calls:

* `recordWorkerSuccess(config.name)` after completion.
* `recordWorkerFailure(config.name, error)` after failure.

Those functions unconditionally update the corresponding `background_jobs` row. That means:

1. Worker A’s lease becomes stale.
2. Worker B reclaims it with token B.
3. Worker A eventually finishes.
4. A’s fenced `releaseJobLock(tokenA)` correctly does nothing.
5. But A’s later **tokenless `recordWorkerSuccess()` or `recordWorkerFailure()` can overwrite B’s running lease state**.

That defeats the fencing guarantee the task is supposed to establish.

Evidence:

* [QueueManager completion/failure hooks](https://github.com/scottpstevenson/Liberty-Bancard-Website-and-CRM/blob/main/server/services/queue-manager.ts)
* [Current lease and health writers](https://github.com/scottpstevenson/Liberty-Bancard-Website-and-CRM/blob/main/server/services/job-registry.ts)

Everything else important in the preflight is confirmed: 24 queue configurations, destructive repeat-schedule reconciliation, lazy initialization callers, zero-like metric failures, stale 23-queue test expectations, skipped/tautological resilience tests, raw DLQ failure exposure, and the duplicate content scheduler.
