STOP if, after this change: (a) the delta chip still renders with zero unit/label text; (b) the sparkline's up-direction and down-direction stroke colors are not wrapped in the same `colorWithAlpha` opacity (still asymmetric); (c) the sparkline is unreadable/too faint on a simulated low-brightness screenshot; (d) the Body Signals pillar case is NOT confirmed as `colors.sky[400]` before starting (if it isn't, stop and flag rather than assuming #6280's state).

## What & Why

Critique P1 item 6 (FitScore hero card) named three concrete, low-risk polish issues in `FitScoreSparkline.tsx` that are addressed here:

- The delta value (e.g. "15" / "-15") has no unit — ambiguous whether it's points, percent, or a raw score delta.
- The sparkline's up-direction stroke line has no opacity reduction while the down-direction line does — this makes the up-trend line visually louder than the down-trend line for no reason, and both are more assertive than a "supporting detail" should be.
- The headline/status text and the sparkline+pillar block sit close together vertically; more breathing room may help hierarchy, but the FitScore card is already dense/tall, so this must be verified against actual height impact via screenshot, not applied as a blind spacing bump.

This is a separate, narrower task split off from the original combined FitScore hero card task. The score-tier reward treatment (critique's "PEAK" badge) could not be located in the files this task touches and is handled by a separate investigation task (see companion task) — no shimmer/animation work is bundled here.

## Done looks like

- The delta chip clearly reads as points (e.g. "15 pts" / "1 pt").
- The sparkline's up and down trend lines use consistent, equally-reduced opacity — neither direction looks more assertive than the other.
- The sparkline remains clearly legible at its new opacity/stroke width.
- Spacing between the headline and sparkline block is only increased if screenshot QA shows it clearly helps hierarchy without pushing the card's total height into unsafe territory (re-checked against #6275's bottom safe-area formula); if it doesn't clearly help, spacing is left unchanged.

## Steps

1. **Verify #6280 landed** — Confirm the Body Signals pillar case returns `colors.sky[400]`. If not, stop and flag rather than proceeding.
2. **Add unit label to the delta chip** — Append a unit suffix to `deltaLabel` (e.g. "15 pts", "1 pt" for the singular case). Confirm the label reads naturally at the chip's existing font size (`typography.difficulty`, 11px); shrink font size slightly if "pts" causes wrapping/truncation rather than leaving it clipped.
3. **Even out sparkline stroke opacity** — Wrap both the up-direction and down-direction sparkline line colors (line 210) in `colorWithAlpha` at the same reduced opacity (e.g. `0.72`, matching the delta chip's existing down-direction value for consistency), and reduce `strokeWidth` from 2 to 1.5 (line 276). Screenshot-verify the line remains legible, including on a simulated low-brightness rendering.
4. **Evaluate spacing conditionally** — Take a screenshot of the card as-is, then a screenshot with `pillarContainer.marginTop` increased modestly (e.g. to 14-16). Only keep the increase if it visibly improves the separation between headline and sparkline block without pushing the card's total height past the safe-area clearance threshold verified in #6275. If it doesn't clearly help, leave `marginTop` unchanged and note that in the PR/summary.
5. **Run gate checks.**
