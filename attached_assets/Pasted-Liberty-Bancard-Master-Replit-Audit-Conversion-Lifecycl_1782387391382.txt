Non-Negotiable Compliance Reality

The system must be designed around compliant channel permissions.

Important rule

Cold scraped leads must not receive automated SMS, AI voice calls, ringless voicemail, autodialed calls, or prerecorded/artificial voice outreach unless Liberty has documented prior express written consent or a counsel-approved lawful basis.

Ringless voicemail must be treated as a regulated automated/prerecorded voice channel, not as a safe cold outreach shortcut.

AI voice must be treated as an artificial/prerecorded voice risk area.

Automated SMS must require documented consent.

Cold B2B email can be used only with CAN-SPAM-compliant controls.

Manual human calls to business lines can be used only through compliance-aware rules, internal DNC, quiet hours, state logic, and opt-out handling.

Do not create any plan that relies on risky automated phone/SMS/voicemail outreach to cold scraped prospects.

Kill Lines

Stop and flag immediately if:

* Any automated SMS can be sent to a cold scraped lead without PEWC.
* Any AI voice call can be made without PEWC.
* Any ringless voicemail can be sent without PEWC.
* Any GHL workflow can bypass Replit’s channel permission state.
* Any opt-out does not immediately suppress future outreach.
* Any DNC contact can be enrolled in a sequence.
* Any form captures phone number but does not preserve consent state.
* Any PEWC checkbox exists but does not store disclosure evidence.
* Any “free processing” page makes unsupported or legally risky claims.
* Any paid landing page lacks conversion tracking.
* Any high-intent page lacks call/booking/upload CTAs.
* Any statement upload succeeds without CRM/deal creation.
* Any appointment booking cannot be attributed to source/campaign.
* Any Closed Won deal does not trigger onboarding.
* Any sensitive document route lacks role protection.
* Any sequence worker sends before checking latest contactability.
* Any queue failure is invisible to operators.
* Any programmatic SEO route is thin, duplicated, or indexable without useful content.
* Any analytics event loses UTM/source attribution.
