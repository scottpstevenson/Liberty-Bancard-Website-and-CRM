## Verdict: **PREFLIGHT REQUIRED**

The copy direction is good, but this is not safe to execute as “rewrite 20 sequences and write them directly to production.”

The task mixes inbound confirmations, nurture, cold SDR outreach, high-intent application reminders, and regulated payment-processing claims. Those do **not** share one copy standard or one compliance boundary.

### Copy corrections

```txt
Do not require every subject to use a curiosity gap.

Use the appropriate subject style by sequence:

- Transactional: clear and expected
- Application completion: direct and action-oriented
- Cold outbound: specific, relevant, low-hype
- Education: benefit or question-led
- Reactivation: contextual and conversational

Do not force social proof into every email.

Use proof only where verified and relevant. Unsupported “client like yours” stories are not acceptable.

Avoid writing all messages around assumed merchant pain such as overcharging unless the sequence context supports it.

Keep one primary idea and one CTA per email.
```

### Kill lines

STOP if unverified savings, pricing, approval, legal, or customer-outcome claims are added.

STOP if transactional confirmations are rewritten like cold sales emails.
