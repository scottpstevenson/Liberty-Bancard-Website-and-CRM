What & Why

The current Liberty Bancard landing page does not feel premium, trustworthy, or polished.

This is not only a mobile issue. The desktop and mobile visual direction both need a serious audit.

The current page has major problems:

* generic dark blue abstract/particle hero background
* white text over a busy image
* weak merchant-finance brand authority
* cluttered top stack
* cheap promo-banner feeling
* repeated disclaimers
* oversized sticky mobile CTA
* chat bubble overlap
* generic icon grids
* excessive whitespace in the wrong places
* mid-page sections that feel like filler
* timeline/process section that feels boring and oversized
* weak conversion hierarchy
* inconsistent premium visual system

The page currently looks like a generic AI/Replit/SaaS template. It does not feel like a serious payments advisory / financial-services brand.

The goal is to create a phased rebuild roadmap that turns it into a premium Liberty Bancard landing page for statement-based processing reviews.

Proposed Page Architecture

Expected structure:

Clean, compact, premium.

Mobile:

* CTA visible above fold
* analysis card below CTA or after compact trust row

Suggested headline options:

Upload a recent statement and Liberty will break down your effective rate, hidden fees, downgrades, and funding timeline — line by line.

Secondary CTA:

Book a 15-Minute Review

Trust row:

Line-item breakdown
Real human support

3. Statement Analysis Preview

Replace abstract background with a polished analysis card showing:

* true effective rate
* fixed monthly fees
* downgrades / card mix
* add-on fees
* funding timeline
* potential savings opportunities

All example values must be clearly illustrative.

4. Sound Familiar?

3–4 compact pain-point cards.

5. What You Get

Professional deliverable-style section.

Show what Liberty gives the merchant:

* effective rate calculation
* fee-by-fee breakdown
* cost driver identification
* comparison options
* funding timeline clarity
* implementation plan

6. How It Works

Compact 3-step process:

1. Upload your statement
2. Liberty breaks down the cost drivers
3. You get options and next steps

No oversized 5-step mobile timeline unless specifically justified.

7. Credibility / Compliance

Compact credibility section, not a giant icon grid.

Possible cards:

* Card-brand aware review
* PCI-aware documentation
* Funding/setup clarity
* Human support

No unsupported certification claims.

8. Final CTA

Premium final CTA card:

Get a Clear Processing Breakdown
Upload your statement and keep the analysis even if you do not switch.

Claims / Compliance Audit

Audit copy for unsupported or risky claims.

Flag:

* guaranteed savings
* zero-cost processing claims
* “beat your rate” claims
* “free terminal” claims
* PCI/card-network claims
* uptime claims
* funding timeline claims
* “as little as 48 hours” claims
* any unqualified absolute promise

For each risky claim, recommend:

keep as-is
rephrase
move near disclaimer
remove
needs legal/business confirmation

Do not remove all compliance language. Make it calmer and better placed.
