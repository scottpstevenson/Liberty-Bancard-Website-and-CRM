2. Make approval and preparation snapshots authoritative
Approval, preflight, and preparation must derive their dependencies server-side from authoritative database state—not trust request-body snapshots.
The immutable dependency snapshot must include:
exact CR-04 cohort run and definition IDs;
status, buildPhase, expiration, member count, membership fingerprint and policy version;
exact CR-04 member/decision IDs and dependency fingerprints;
approved program, sequence, ordered steps and content-version hashes;
claim/evidence registry versions and expiration;
sender identity, From address and reply route;
compliance footer and unsubscribe configuration;
provider readiness/circuit state;
applicable campaign, sender, provider and canary caps;
global/channel pause epoch as observational evidence;
renderer version and any prompt/model/output hashes.
Use canonical serialization and a stable hash. Approval and preparation must compare-and-set the exact open gate revision. Any changed, missing, stale, expired, unavailable, or mismatched dependency must block preparation.
Preparation must never silently expand or recompute cohort membership.
3. Complete atomic preparation and reservation behavior
Within one database transaction:
lock the approved package/gate and frozen cohort;
revalidate the exact dependency snapshot;
enforce the 250-member maximum;
create each held delivery intent exactly once;
create held manual-task intents exactly once;
write the preparation receipt;
close/consume the applicable gate revision.
Add database uniqueness fences for concurrent preparation and per-recipient/per-step duplication.
Do not create live CR-05 authority tasks during preparation. A held manual-task intent may only materialize through storage.createAuthorityTask() after its future prerequisite delivery event, using a stable idempotency key.
Implement immutable reservation records for the required sender, campaign, provider, minute/hour/day and canary limits, including expiration and reconciliation. Because dispatch is disabled, preparation must not consume a send reservation or provider attempt.
