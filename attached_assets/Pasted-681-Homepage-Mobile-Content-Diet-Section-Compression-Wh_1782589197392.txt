681 — Homepage Mobile Content Diet + Section Compression

What & Why

The homepage mobile architecture is still too dense after the rhythm repair. The section backgrounds improved, but the mobile page still renders desktop-depth modules as long stacked sections: review contents, “yours to keep,” dual timelines, calculator fallback, strategy accordion, verticals, rate comparison, and full Why Liberty cards.

This creates a 20+ screen mobile page even though the content is mostly repeating the same core idea: upload a statement, Liberty reviews the fees, then presents options before switching.

This task compresses mobile-only content density without redesigning desktop.

The goal is not to remove important selling points. The goal is to make mobile feel like a premium conversion page instead of a documentation page.

⸻

Done Looks Like

At 390px mobile:

* Homepage reads as a clear mobile story, not a stacked desktop page.
* No single mid-page section consumes more than ~1.25 mobile screens unless it is the hero or final CTA.
* “What Your Liberty Review Includes” is compressed into a compact mobile summary card, not a tall 6-row report table.
* “You Keep the Breakdown” is removed as a standalone mobile card and merged into the compact review summary or risk-reversal language.
* “How The Liberty Analysis Works” is compressed on mobile into one compact process card.
* The 5-step onboarding timeline is collapsed on mobile or shown as a short “After approval” row, not a full second timeline.
* Rate Calculator remains desktop-only; mobile shows only a compact CTA row/link to /estimate.
* Verticals section is compressed on mobile to a compact industry list/chip grid, not mixed accordion plus large standalone cards.
* “Why Merchants Switch” rate comparison card is desktop-only or replaced on mobile with a short summary CTA.
* Why Liberty feature cards are compact on mobile, not large full-width stacked cards.
* StickyMobileCTA remains navy and keeps existing IntersectionObserver trigger logic.
* Desktop layout at md: and above is unchanged unless explicitly noted.
* Upload/book CTA destinations and tracking calls remain unchanged.

Steps

1. Compress “What Your Liberty Review Includes” on mobile

Current issue: the mobile section renders like a full report document with 6 large rows, then an additional “You Keep the Breakdown” card. This consumes multiple screens.

Desktop: keep existing report-style module.

Mobile: replace with a compact card.

Required mobile structure:

The Liberty Review Includes
A clear line-by-line look at your current processing costs.
✓ Effective rate
✓ Fee drivers
✓ Downgrades
✓ Add-ons
✓ Funding timeline
✓ Options summary
You keep the breakdown either way.
[Upload Statement — Free]

Implementation:

* Keep desktop version as hidden md:block.
* Add mobile-only version as md:hidden.
* Use one compact card, not 6 large rows.
* Use a 2-column bullet grid if it fits cleanly; otherwise use compact stacked rows.
* Remove the separate “Yours to Keep” mobile card.
* Keep any compliance note, but make it small and attached to the compact card.

Kill line:

Stop if the mobile Review Includes section still renders as six large full-width rows plus a separate keep-the-breakdown card.

⸻

2. Compress “How The Liberty Analysis Works” on mobile

Current issue: mobile shows a large 3-step process, then a full 5-step onboarding timeline. This reads as 8 sequential steps and consumes multiple screens.

Desktop: keep the richer process/timeline layout.

Mobile: replace with one compact process card.

Required mobile structure:

How It Works
1. Upload your statement
2. We review the fee lines
3. You get clear options
After approval: setup, terminal shipping, and first batch are tracked by Liberty.

Implementation:

* Keep desktop process/timeline as hidden md:block.
* Add mobile-only compact version as md:hidden.
* The mobile version should fit around one screen.
* Do not show giant numbered circles on mobile.
* Do not show the full 5-step onboarding timeline on mobile.
* Preserve the existing compliance/timeline disclaimer in small text if needed.

Kill line:

Stop if mobile still shows both the 3-step analysis timeline and the full 5-step onboarding timeline.

⸻

3. Keep mobile Rate Calculator as CTA-only

Current issue: the Quick Estimate section still appears as a large mobile chapter and contributes to page length.

Required:

* Desktop calculator remains unchanged.
* Mobile should be a compact CTA band only.

Mobile copy:

Want a quick estimate?
Run the 60-second rate check using your volume and monthly fees.
[Open Rate Check]

Requirements:

* No form fields on mobile homepage.
* No estimate preview card on mobile homepage.
* Link to /estimate.
* Must fit in less than half a screen.

Kill line:

Stop if the mobile homepage renders calculator inputs, estimate preview cards, or rate-range cards inside the calculator section.

⸻

4. Keep Choose Path accordion, but reduce vertical weight

Current issue: Choose Path accordion is directionally correct, but the expanded first item is very tall.

Mobile requirements:

* Keep accordion.

* Keep CTA inside expanded item.
* Remove extra illustrative structure blocks from mobile if they make the accordion too tall.
* Desktop cards unchanged.

Kill line:

Stop if the first expanded strategy item takes more than roughly one full mobile screen by itself.
