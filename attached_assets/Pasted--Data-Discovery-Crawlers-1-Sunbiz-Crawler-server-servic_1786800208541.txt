🔍 Data Discovery / Crawlers
1. Sunbiz Crawler (server/services/sunbiz-enrichment.ts)
The biggest source of raw contacts — 1.9M Florida business registry entities. For each entity it:

Scrapes the Sunbiz detail page for officers and registered agents
Hits Serper (Google Search API) for website, email, phone
Scrapes the business website + contact/about/team pages
Falls back to Facebook → Yelp → YellowPages → LinkedIn → BBB
Runs a DBPR license lookup
Does a final Serper email discovery pass
Deduplicates emails/phones found across all sources
Runs AI classification to pick the best email/phone from everything found
Auto-converts hot/warm enriched entities into prospects if they have contact details
2. SERPER Web Search (server/services/serper.ts, server/services/sdr/serper-enrichment.ts)
Used in three places:

Inside Sunbiz enrichment (business + contact + email search passes)
In general prospect enrichment (if website/email/phone is missing)
As a standalone SDR enrichment: hits Google Places for website, phone, address, rating, review count; then searches for email patterns on the domain
3. LinkedIn Enrichment (server/services/linkedin-enrichment.ts)
A separate enrichment module — UI exists at ContactDetail → LinkedinEnrichmentSection. Writes back to the contact record.

4. SDR Sub-Enrichment Modules
Four specialized crawlers under server/services/sdr/:

rdap-enrichment.ts — domain registration / WHOIS data
jsonld-enrichment.ts — structured data extraction from website pages (JSON-LD schema)
contactpage-enrichment.ts — dedicated scraper for /contact pages
re-enrichment.ts — re-runs enrichment when data goes stale or is incomplete
5. Optional Third-Party Providers
Configured but not always active: Outscraper, Apify, Apollo — imported via integration-validator.ts. CSV imports also normalize data from these formats.

🤖 AI Enrichment
6. GPT-4o-mini Business Analyzer (server/services/enrichment.ts:65-124 — enrichWithAI())
Takes company name, email, phone, location, current vertical, and website text → asks GPT to return:

Canonical vertical classification
Revenue range estimate
Business description
Pain-point tags
Hot / Warm / Cold / Unqualified score + reason
7. AI Website Quality Scorer (server/services/sdr/scoring.ts:625-659 — aiAssessWebsiteQuality())
GPT-4o-mini rates the business website on maturity and quality — used as a signal in the SDR scoring pipeline.

8. Decision-Maker Confidence (server/services/enrichment.ts:126-147)
Rule-based, not AI. Looks at the contact's job title keywords and assigns confidence levels: 95 / 80 / 60 / 40 / 0. Written to isDecisionMaker and decisionMakerConfidence columns.

9. AI Lead Segmentation (server/routes/lead-ops.ts:193-305)
Admin-triggered. OpenAI analyzes the current lead pool and returns segment recommendations — accessible from the Lead Ops Center.

10. NBA Explanation (server/services/nba-service.ts:402-431)
GPT writes a 1–2 sentence explanation after the NBA action is already determined by rules. Does not drive the score itself.

📊 Scoring Engines
11. Data Readiness Score (server/services/contact-readiness.ts)
Pure field-completeness score. Max 100.

Signal	Points
Valid non-placeholder email	25
Real company name	15
Canonical vertical classification	15
Phone present + valid type	15
First name	10
City	5
2-letter state	5
Valid website	5
Last name	5
Grades: A ≥80 / B ≥60 / C ≥40 / D ≥20 / F. Written to dataReadinessScore, dataReadinessGrade, readinessBreakdown. Does not include consent, behavior, or lead intent — pure data quality only. Recomputed in a BullMQ queue via enqueueReadinessRecalculation().

12. Lead Score (server/services/lead-scoring.ts)
Behavioral + commercial intent score. Max 100.

Component	Max
Revenue potential (from deal/contact fields)	30
Switchability (how likely to switch processors)	25
Underwriting confidence (deal quality)	25
Engagement (email replies, call connections, sequence membership)	20
Quiz bonus (if they completed a savings quiz)	20
Offer confidence bonus	bonus
Written to leadScore, revPotentialScore, switchabilityScore, uwConfidenceScore, engagementScore, scoreBreakdown. Also writes deal priority/tier. Recomputed via the same BullMQ queue worker.

13. Sunbiz Hot/Warm/Cold Score (AI-assigned)
Different from lead score — assigned by enrichWithAI() during Sunbiz enrichment. Stored on the Sunbiz entity and written back to the linked contact/prospect during writebackEnrichmentToLinkedRecords(). Controls auto-conversion eligibility.

14. Churn Score (server/services/churn-score.ts)
For existing merchants only (post-activation). Six signal families:

Signal	Max Points
Processing volume decline (10/25/50%)	30
Chargeback ratio / trend	25
Support ticket velocity	20
NPS score	25
Merchant portal inactivity	20
No reply to outreach sequences	15
Weighted, normalized 0–100. Tiers: Critical >85 / High >70 / Medium ≥40 / Low. Fires openSaveCaseIfNeeded() automatically at High/Critical.

🔄 Contactability / Send-Gate Scoring
15. Contactability Fence (server/services/contactability.ts)
Not a score — a pass/fail gate evaluated per channel (email, SMS, voice_ai, manual_call, ringless_vm) before every send. Checks: consent tier, lifecycle state, DNC flags, email status, phone type, state time-of-day, daily rate limits, business hours. Returns blocked/allowed + reason. Writes enforcement audit logs for automated channels.

16. ZeroBounce Email Status (server/services/sdr/zerobounce.ts)
Writes email_status → valid / unsafe / unverified / unknown. The fix in #1533 makes unvalidated the default for new contacts. This is what #1513 batch-sweeps across the existing population.

🔁 Lifecycle State Machine (server/services/LifecycleService.ts)
27 states (cold_lead → engaged → converted → churned etc.). Drives NBA actions, sequence targeting, and contactability decisions. Transitions are event-driven (form submission, email reply, call connection, deal creation, etc.) — not a score but positions each contact on the pipeline board.
