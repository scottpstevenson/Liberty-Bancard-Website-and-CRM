What & Why

The current Home page has one major failure:

It feels like one endless white/off-white page with bordered cards dropped onto it.

The hero improved, but the rest of the page does not feel like a premium website. It lacks rhythm, contrast, visual momentum, and deliberate section breaks.

Current problems visible in screenshots:

* page is mostly one continuous white/off-white canvas
* section breaks are weak or invisible
* bg-muted/30 reads as white and does not create contrast
* too many sections use the same white card + thin border formula
* the report/audit visual system mostly stops after the hero
* the page feels long, flat, and generated
* there is no premium pacing between sections
* some sections feel like they belong to different pages
* internal platform section interrupts the merchant statement-review story
* verticals grid is six identical boxes
* offer cards are sparse and uneven
* calculator lacks pull
* rate comparison is too visually buried
* duplicate testimonial / merchant story sections render
* first video-placeholder testimonial section looks unfinished and must be removed
* several claims need safer wording
* final CTA does not yet feel decisive enough

Do not grade relative to the old page. Grade against premium financial-services websites.
