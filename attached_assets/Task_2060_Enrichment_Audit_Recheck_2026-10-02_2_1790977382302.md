## Executive result

The publish fixed several real defects, but the system still does not reliably convert its large pool into **one primary and at most two alternate qualified recipients per business**. There are 153,566 active CRM contacts with email, 85,416 canonical businesses, only **32 verified linked email contacts** across canonical businesses, and **31 in frozen v2 SFP cohorts**. A concrete live contact that appears repeatedly in the ZeroBounce-valid named review queue has **no associated company and “Email Validation: Not yet validated”** on its CRM page. Its putative business page labels it a *potential contact candidate, not a verified link*, and has no master lead. The business/domain pairing itself warrants corroboration. A validation receipt alone cannot establish employer identity or outreach eligibility.

At a hard maximum of three recipients per business, 5,000 distinct recipients require at least **1,667 businesses**; at one or two average, 2,500–5,000 businesses. Repeated 100-business cohorts cannot meet that objective alone. The code still has no shared transactional three-per-business reservation across contact/free/paid sources.

## Remaining causes and exact corrections

### October 2 follow-up: the operator loop is a product defect

The live mobile screen shows run `130b48c0-e106-4baf-97ca-5288ae7c71e6` at **150 scanned, 47 suggestions, one ambiguous, 103 unmatched, cursor 243**, then asks the user to “Resume next bounded batch.” [`contact-business-reconciliation.ts:391–394`](https://github.com/scottpstevenson/Liberty-Bancard-Website-and-CRM/blob/74c15805a5cbdb3e0a82183c8c56104999075671/server/services/contact-business-reconciliation.ts#L391-L394) explicitly makes one keyset page per operator click and sets `ready` until another request. The maximum batch is 100. Scanning ~153,566 contacts this way would need over 1,500 manual requests, and [the worker only records candidate suggestions](https://github.com/scottpstevenson/Liberty-Bancard-Website-and-CRM/blob/74c15805a5cbdb3e0a82183c8c56104999075671/server/services/contact-business-reconciliation.ts#L306-L317). Those 47 suggestions are not verified links. The newer [corroborated matcher](https://github.com/scottpstevenson/Liberty-Bancard-Website-and-CRM/blob/74c15805a5cbdb3e0a82183c8c56104999075671/server/services/contact-business-corroborated-links.ts#L91-L141) writes a verified decision only through an admin confirmation route. The screenshot is therefore showing intended code behavior, not a missed setting.

**Replace this with a continuous reconciliation service:** one initial program authorization starts a durable scheduler/worker that drains bounded pages automatically, checkpoints each page, resumes after restart/publish, leases work to prevent duplicate processing, and stops only at a genuine terminal condition or an explicit pause. New/changed contacts and businesses enter an incremental queue after the backfill. The UI should show progress, rate, yielded verified links, reasons, retries and a pause control; “Resume next batch” becomes recovery only.

The worker should classify each unique candidate as (a) **automatic verified link** under a versioned, independently corroborated, conflict-free rule; (b) **exception review** for ambiguous, copied-domain or conflicting evidence; or (c) **unmatched/deferred** with a next evidence action. Implement a dedicated service principal and canonical decision-writer authority for (a), with immutable evidence, snapshot hash, revision compare-and-swap and audit trail. Do not impersonate an admin reviewer or silently turn name-only suggestions into verified links. A bounded dry run, independently sampled false-link rate, and invariant tests precede activation; then the worker advances without one click per contact. Manual review capacity is reserved for a small, ranked exception queue.

## Highest-return bulk enrichment route

Build a **durable, resumable business queue**, initially from the 2,496 eligible SFP targets. Prioritize expected *new verified recipient yield per marginal call*, not merely a name-derived vertical score or “never frozen.” Across each business:

| Rank | Current evidence | Next action | Stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Target geography/vertical, unambiguous canonical identity, existing CRM person/email and independent employer evidence | Automatically bind through audited policy; reuse fresh matching ZB receipt or validate best address; stage primary | One distinct qualified primary; at most two alternates |
| 2 | Trusted website/domain and first-party role or named contact | Deduplicate; validate best candidate; commit if employer and policy pass | Invalid/unsafe, conflict, or three slots filled |
| 3 | Strong fit and verified business, no contact/site | One bounded business/site discovery, then website email extraction; validate only best 1–3 | Low-confidence domain, no relevant address, or poor measured cost/yield |
| 4 | Name-only, unresolved geography or identity conflict | Resolve independent cheap evidence or defer | No provider fan-out until business path is credible |

Pilot **100 distinct businesses across five verticals and three counties** in keyset batches of 25–100. Use persistent IDs, idempotent replays, fresh receipt reuse, explicit retry/terminal states and pause/resume. Report businesses attempted, distinct qualified, per-business 0/1/2/3 distribution, verified link IDs, current ZB receipt IDs, ready-held intents, holds and recorded provider cost versus invoices. Sample false links independently. Scale only after measured yield per marginal call and actual recipient quality justify it. Outbound remains paused.

**Acceptance:** each successful record traces source → canonical business → verified employer link → current validation receipt → policy eligibility → one of three business slots → master lead → ready-held intent → paused enrollment. The present UI lacks a trusted single bulk mode satisfying this definition.
