
My answers to the open questions
1. Design direction
Approve the light-first statement-document concept.
That is exactly the right direction:
light-first financial services
navy as authority accent
statement-analysis preview as the hero visual
no particle/wave/AI background
no cheap promo banner
This will make Liberty feel like a payments advisory firm instead of a dark SaaS template.
2. Headline
Use:
See What Your Processor Is Really Charging
That is the strongest headline. It is direct, merchant-specific, and curiosity-driven without making a guaranteed savings claim.
The backup option:
Turn Your Processing Statement Into a Clear Cost Breakdown
is safer but more boring. I’d use it as the subheadline idea, not the main headline.
3. 99.9% uptime
Remove it unless you have a real SLA/source backing it.
Replace with something like:
Reliable processing support
or:
Processing clarity without the guesswork
4. “Cancel Anytime / No ETF / No Penalty”
Do not keep that as a big claim unless it is 100% true across the actual merchant agreement.
Safer replacement:
Clear terms before you switch.
or:
No surprises in the proposal.
5. Statement preview card
Keep illustrative numbers, but make them feel like a clean sample report, not a fake dashboard promise.
Use labels like:
Sample statement review
Illustrative only
Actual findings require statement review
.
