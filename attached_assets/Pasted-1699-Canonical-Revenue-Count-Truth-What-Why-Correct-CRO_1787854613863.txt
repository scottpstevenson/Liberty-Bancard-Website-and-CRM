1699 - Canonical Revenue Count Truth

What & Why

Correct CRO-01’s implementation contract before any code changes. The live repository confirms that People, Prospect Staging, Leads, Deals, and Merchants lack one executable read contract: archived and filtered People counts drift from rows, prospect and Pipeline metrics are capped client snapshots, revenue consumers use different deal populations, Portfolio is not merchant-only, and prospect conversion can race or emit unequal side effects.

Build one focused read authority and harden the existing mutation authorities without copying records, inventing ownership, or creating competing writers. Every defect, edge case, test, gate, and kill line below is a direct build requirement or an explicit scope boundary.

Done looks like

People is the canonical contact set; Prospect Staging is separate; Lead and Merchant are non-synonymous states over contacts and may overlap
People rows, totals, facets, filters, sorting, and pagination use one server predicate and exclude archived contacts by default
Lead means one distinct eligible contact with at least one qualifying open sales deal; multiple deals do not inflate Lead counts
Pipeline and task-owned Reporting consumers share production, archive, pipeline, stage, ownership, and snapshot semantics
Prospect Staging is consistently admin/manager-only because the current schema has no sales-owner field
Prospect updates reject unknown and authority-managed fields
Single and batch prospect conversion reuse one contact and one intended open sales deal through the existing deal authority
Portfolio includes only distinct eligible contacts with at least one active, activated MID and reports active MID count separately
List responses expose truthful totals, filters, scope, pagination, and real snapshot time
Reconciliation is aggregate-only and restricted to admin/manager
Safe deal failures carry a stable code and correlation ID without PII or SQL details
Focused tests prove role isolation, cardinality, pagination, concurrency, side-effect denial, and error redaction
Out of scope

Production reads, classification decisions, deduplication, backfills, cleanup, migrations, deployment, or exact-SHA runtime certification
GHL/provider mutation, campaign activation, enrollment-policy changes, or outreach
A new prospect ownership column or agent Prospect Staging policy
MID lifecycle changes such as clearing historical activation timestamps
Broad dashboard null-safety, detailed Pipeline retry UX, task remediation, or full client/server diagnostics
CRO-02 classification/provenance remediation and later CRO tranches

Concurrency and side-effect contract

Authorize the prospect under admin/manager policy.
Acquire or reuse the fenced prospect claim.
Create or reuse the contact through writeContact in local-first mode.
Persist the recovery contact ID under the claim.
In one transaction, lock the canonical contact/stable key, reuse or create one intended open sales deal through the transaction-aware deal owner, and finalize only while claim ownership remains valid.
Commit canonical local audit facts with that transaction.
After commit, run the existing effect adapter. Tests replace it with recording no-ops and deny every provider, queue, task, notification, and enrollment transport.
Failure injection is required between every durable step. Claim loss must roll back the deal/finalization unit and replay must return the same IDs.
