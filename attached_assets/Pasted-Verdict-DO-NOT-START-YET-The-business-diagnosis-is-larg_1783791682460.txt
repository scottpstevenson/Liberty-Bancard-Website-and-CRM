Verdict: DO NOT START YET

The business diagnosis is largely right: the 154K CRM contacts are the immediately actionable pool, while campaigns are still prospect-centric and the current behavioral score is unsuitable for imported cold outreach.

But the proposed three-task plan is still too broad and makes several unsafe architecture jumps. Do not retire #961/#962 or start rewriting the campaign engine yet.

What is correct

* CRM contacts should become the primary operational audience for outreach.
* Imported records need a data-readiness signal, not a behavioral-engagement score.
* Campaign targeting needs contact-based vertical selection and scalable batching.
* Registry/entity enrichment needs a controlled promotion path into CRM.
* Pipeline deals should represent actual sales work, not every discovered business.

False assumptions and risks

1. Completeness score should not replace lead_score.
    A record with email, phone, and company data is campaign-ready—not necessarily a “hot lead.” Calling 134K cold records “Hot” would corrupt sales-priority semantics.
2. Having an email does not make a contact sendable.
    Campaign eligibility must still pass canonical contactability, suppression, DNC, unsubscribe, bounce, consent/legal basis, duplicate enrollment, and sender-cap checks.
3. “No score gate” is unsafe wording.
    Remove the behavioral-score gate, but replace it with a formal outreach readiness eligibility contract, not unrestricted enrollment.
4. 152K sends cannot be treated as one campaign fire.
    It needs preview, audience snapshots, approval, rate limits, cancellation, resumability, idempotency, sender allocation, and reply/unsubscribe handling.
5. Auto-enrollment after enrichment is dangerous.
    Enrichment may create/update CRM records, but it should not automatically send or enroll without an explicitly approved campaign rule and contactability check.
6. Sunbiz and prospects cannot simply be declared redundant.
    They currently own discovery/enrichment history and prospect workflows. Deprecation requires a field-by-field ownership and migration audit.
7. Email → phone → company/city dedupe is not automatically safe.
    Shared business phones, generic inboxes, franchises, multi-location companies, and reused domains can cause destructive merges.

Recommended execution sequence after the audit

Phase 1 — CRM Campaign Audience Enablement
Allow existing campaign infrastructure to target reviewed CRM contacts by vertical through the canonical contactability and enrollment paths. No mass architecture consolidation yet.

Phase 2 — Data Completeness + Outreach Readiness
Add a separate readiness projection and backfill it across CRM contacts. Do not rename it Hot/Warm or overwrite the current lead score.

Phase 3 — Universal Intake and Enrichment Routing
Normalize all source ingestion into CRM with source provenance, safe dedupe, enrichment eligibility, and controlled promotion.

Phase 4 — Table and Navigation Consolidation
Only after ownership evidence proves prospects or discovery tables can be migrated/deprecated safely.

Kill lines

STOP if data completeness replaces lead_score or labels imported contacts “Hot.”
STOP if CRM campaign targeting bypasses contactability, DNC, suppression,
unsubscribe, bounce, or consent checks.
STOP if enrichment automatically enrolls or sends without an approved campaign rule.
STOP if a 100K+ audience can be launched without preview, snapshot, pause,
cancel, resume, rate limits, and duplicate prevention.
STOP if a second campaign queue or enrollment owner is created.
STOP if prospects or sunbiz_entities are deprecated without a complete
reader/writer and relationship audit.
STOP if dedupe can merge records solely on shared phone or loose company matching.
STOP if API-direct Outscraper, Apollo, and Apify integrations are bundled into
one implementation task without separate provider contracts and rate-limit reviews.

Final status: PREFLIGHT REQUIRED

The immediate priority is correct: make existing CRM contacts safely targetable by vertical. But frame it as CRM audience enablement through existing canonical send/enrollment systems—not a wholesale “Campaign Engine v2” rewrite or a replacement of behavioral scoring with field-completeness points.
