674 — Homepage Redesign Recovery Audit: Why the Last Pass Failed

Mode

READ-ONLY AUDIT ONLY.

Do not edit code. Do not build. Do not patch copy yet.

The last homepage redesign did not work. This task must diagnose why, decide what to keep/revert, and produce a corrected homepage architecture before any new build pass.

⸻

Current Verdict

NOT APPROVED.

The latest homepage pass improved some individual words, but it did not fix the core product-positioning or mobile/desktop architecture.

The page still reads like:

a free statement review / fee audit funnel

instead of:

a premium credit card processing and merchant services company

The statement review should be the entry offer. It should not be the center of the entire company identity.

⸻

What Failed

1. Hero still centers the statement too much

Current hero:

Merchant Services Built Around Your Statement

This includes “Merchant Services,” but the actual concept is still statement-first.

A visitor still reads:

This company reviews statements.

not:

This company handles my credit card processing, merchant account, terminals, POS setup, funding, and support.

Audit whether the hero should move to a stronger category-first line, such as:

Credit Card Processing for Businesses That Want Clearer Rates and Real Support

or:

Merchant Services, Terminal Setup, and Statement-Based Pricing Review

or:

Payment Processing Built for Local Businesses — Reviewed Line by Line

The homepage must establish the business category before it sells the free review.

⸻

2. Mobile H1 is too large and uncontrolled

The mobile hero headline consumes most of the first screen.

Audit:

* mobile H1 font size
* line height
* max width
* word breaks
* contrast between title and subhead
* whether Source Serif is too heavy at this size
* whether the blue-highlighted phrase creates awkward emphasis

A premium mobile hero should feel composed, not oversized.

⸻

3. Sticky mobile CTA is too dominant

The sticky blue “Upload Statement — Free” bar appears through the page and makes the experience feel like an aggressive app funnel.

Audit:

* should the dock appear later?
* should it be dark navy instead of bright blue?
* should it reduce height?
* should it be hidden on dense/content sections?
* should it include Book as a secondary action?
* does it cover section endings or CTA areas?
* does it make the site feel less premium?

The sticky CTA should support conversion, not dominate every screen.

⸻

4. Section breaks are still weak

The page still feels like a long sequence of cards on white/off-white backgrounds.

Audit every section and classify whether it has a true visual chapter break.

A real homepage needs fewer, stronger chapters:

Hero
Core services
Statement review / diagnostic
Processing options
Setup / terminal implementation
Proof / case studies
Final CTA

Not 20+ semi-equal sections.

⸻

5. Mobile page is still too dense

Mobile still has too many large sections:

* huge hero
* huge audit card
* large trust strip
* 2x2 pain cards
* verticals accordion plus extra cards
* terminal gallery
* setup cards
* rate comparison card
* giant proof cards
* guarantee card
* testimonial card
* sticky CTA throughout

Audit which mobile sections should be:

kept
compressed
accordion
carousel
desktop-only
removed
moved below final CTA

Target mobile homepage should not require 20+ screenshots.

⸻

6. Testimonials / savings claims are risky

Current testimonial language includes phrases like:

processing cost dropped to nearly zero
saving us $340 a month
we were paying Square over $1,100 a month

These are high-risk claims unless documented and substantiated.

Audit testimonial/case-study section for:

* unsupported savings claims
* “nearly zero” language
* competitor-specific claims
* implied guaranteed outcomes
* missing case-study qualification
* mismatch with “No savings claims without statement review”

Recommend safer testimonial framing or removal until documentation exists.

⸻

7. Processing company identity is still underdeveloped

The site needs to clearly show Liberty provides:

credit card processing
merchant accounts
interchange-plus pricing
terminal setup
POS setup
virtual/keyed payments
funding support
human account support
statement-based review

Audit whether those are visible early enough and whether the homepage feels like a full provider, not just an analysis tool.
