Weekly lead generation model

A. Nightly acquisition jobs

Run every night by vertical + metro.

Example searches:
	•	“auto repair miami fl”
	•	“med spa tampa fl”
	•	“dentist orlando fl”
	•	“chiropractor fort lauderdale fl”

Per job flow:
	1.	Pull business records from Maps/directories
	2.	Normalize business name/address/phone/site
	3.	Check if already in businesses
	4.	Insert net new businesses
	5.	Queue enrichment

A realistic first pass is:
	•	200–500 businesses per vertical per week
	•	5–6 verticals
	•	3–5 markets each

That gets you into the 1,000–5,000/week range.

For each business:

Scoring model I’d use

Use a 100-point weighted score.

Fit score — 25

How good is the merchant type?
	•	med spa: high
	•	dental: high
	•	auto repair: high
	•	restaurant: medium-high
	•	solo low-volume service business: lower

Revenue potential — 20

Signals:
	•	review count
	•	multi-location
	•	ad activity
	•	site quality
	•	number of employees/pages/services

Reachability — 15
	•	valid email
	•	valid mobile
	•	website present
	•	contact page present

Priority — 15
	•	target state
	•	vertical focus this month
	•	strategic region

Processor opportunity — 15
	•	Square
	•	Stripe
	•	Toast
	•	Clover
	•	Shopify
	•	PayPal
	•	NCR
	•	unknown processor but clear card acceptance

Growth signals — 10
	•	active ads
	•	recent reviews
	•	multiple new locations
	•	hiring
	•	active social presence

Routing:
	•	85+ = hot
	•	70–84 = warm
	•	55–69 = nurture
	•	under 55 = hold/re-enrich later

Processor detection layer

This is one of the highest ROI additions.

You want to identify merchants likely using:
	•	Square
	•	Stripe
	•	Clover
	•	Toast
	•	Shopify
	•	PayPal
	•	Mindbody
	•	Vagaro
	•	Boulevard

Detection methods:
	•	website scripts
	•	checkout page assets
	•	booking platform embeds
	•	POS references on careers/help pages
	•	receipts/payment FAQ pages
	•	builtwith-style fingerprints

This lets Liberty message like:
	•	“If you’re on Square, we may be able to lower effective cost.”
	•	“If you’re processing high ticket med spa volume, a custom setup often beats flat-rate pricing.”

That is far stronger than generic “lower your fees” messaging.

What’s still missing from your current plan

These are the biggest gaps to close.

1. Canonical business identity

You need one true business record.

Use a business fingerprint like:
	•	normalized business name
	•	normalized street
	•	phone
	•	domain

Without this, your system will duplicate leads constantly.

2. Global suppression layer

One merchant says stop once, they must be blocked everywhere:
	•	email
	•	SMS
	•	call
	•	chat
	•	future imports

3. Source attribution

You need to know where every lead came from:
	•	outscraper
	•	apify-yelp
	•	apify-facebook
	•	seo
	•	affiliate
	•	chat widget
	•	manual upload

This matters for ROI and lead quality decisions.

4. Re-enrichment cycle

A dead lead today may become good later.

Recheck every 60–90 days:
	•	email found?
	•	website improved?
	•	ads now running?
	•	new phone?
	•	new reviews?
	•	multi-location expansion?

5. Closed-won lookalike model

Once you have enough wins, create a “best merchant profile” and prioritize matching businesses.

That will compound results over time.
