## Verdict

Do not publish this homepage as-is.

The screenshots are not showing isolated polish problems. They expose a broken public-site theme architecture, an overlong homepage, weak visual hierarchy, repeated spreadsheet/report motifs, and several conversion and credibility issues.

The biggest defect is deterministic:

* Dark mode changes `--primary` from navy to almost white.
* Homepage sections use `bg-primary` while hard-coding `text-white`.
* Result: white text on white backgrounds.
* Dark-mode `--accent` is nearly the same color as the dark page background.
* Result: navy/blue text disappears on navy.

The calculated contrast ratios are:

* Dark accent on dark background: **1.22:1**
* White text on dark-mode primary: **1.05:1**
* Required for normal text: **4.5:1**

This originates in [`index.css`](https://github.com/scottpstevenson/Liberty-Bancard-Website-and-CRM/blob/10d2101223ea026038d007682658aad6f781ac1c/client/src/index.css#L67-L88) and the system-driven default in [`ThemeProvider.tsx`](https://github.com/scottpstevenson/Liberty-Bancard-Website-and-CRM/blob/10d2101223ea026038d007682658aad6f781ac1c/client/src/components/ThemeProvider.tsx#L14-L41).

## Global design problems

### 1. The public website should not inherit dashboard/system dark mode

Recommended decision: make all marketing routes use a fixed light brand theme. Keep dark mode only for the CRM/dashboard.

Do not try to repair this by adding more scattered `dark:` classes. That will perpetuate the same failure.

### 2. The blue top toolbar should be removed

[`Navbar.tsx`](https://github.com/scottpstevenson/Liberty-Bancard-Website-and-CRM/blob/10d2101223ea026038d007682658aad6f781ac1c/client/src/components/Navbar.tsx#L194-L367) currently has:

* A separate `bg-primary` phone/email strip
* A second navigation row
* A public theme toggle
* Two nav CTAs
* Partner and staff links competing with buyer navigation

Replace it with one 68–72px white header:

* Logo
* Solutions
* Industries
* Resources
* Partners
* Phone as a restrained utility link
* “Book a Call” secondary action
* “Free Statement Review” navy primary button

Remove the public theme toggle and move Staff Login to the footer or utility menu.

### 3. The homepage is structurally far too long

`Home.tsx` is **1,731 lines, 119 KB, and contains 20 `<section>` elements**. Desktop shows approximately 18; mobile still shows approximately 17.

That is the direct cause of the “one endless page” feeling. The earlier repository audit explicitly set a maximum of ten major sections, but the current implementation violates that requirement.

### 4. Too many report/spreadsheet motifs

Ledger lines, dots, grids, audit stamps, report chips, tables, reference numbers, and administrative cards are used repeatedly.

The pattern is not limited to the homepage: ledger/grid/dot textures occur across at least 17 public-facing files, including `PublicHero`, `FreeAnalysis`, `SavingsPage`, `RateComparison`, industry pages, and `AboutContact`.

Remove them from the public design system. They make the brand feel like accounting software rather than a premium merchant-services company.

### 5. Typography is over-styled and undersized

The marketing CSS forces Source Serif onto every `h1`, `h2`, and `h3`. This makes small card titles look editorial and inconsistent.

The homepage also contains:

* 17 instances of 10px text
* 9 instances of 11px text
* 61 `text-xs` instances

Use Source Serif only for major H1/H2 display copy. Keep card headings and interface labels in IBM Plex Sans. Minimum sizes:

* H1: 56/60 desktop, 36/40 mobile
* H2: 40/48 desktop, 30/36 mobile
* Body: 16/26
* Supporting text: 14/22
* Legal/microcopy: never below 12px

### 6. Shared CTA markup needs repair

`Home.tsx` contains roughly 40 `Link → Button` structures. The shared [`PublicHero`](https://github.com/scottpstevenson/Liberty-Bancard-Website-and-CRM/blob/10d2101223ea026038d007682658aad6f781ac1c/client/src/components/public/PublicHero.tsx) repeats the same pattern.

Use `Button asChild` with the link inside it. Avoid nested interactive elements.

### 7. Main and SSR homepage disagree

The server-rendered homepage still says “Stop Overpaying for Merchant Payment Processing,” while the React homepage says “Credit Card Processing Without the Rate Games.” It also presents different statistics and sections.

That mismatch is visible in [`server/ssr/home.ts`](https://github.com/scottpstevenson/Liberty-Bancard-Website-and-CRM/blob/10d2101223ea026038d007682658aad6f781ac1c/server/ssr/home.ts#L48-L72), and the older version is what the [current public HTML](https://libertybancard.com/) exposes to crawlers.

This creates SEO inconsistency, potential layout shift during hydration, and conflicting claims. The SSR and client homepage must share one canonical content model.

## Recommended premium homepage architecture

Reduce the page to nine chapters:

1. **Hero**
   Clear category positioning, two CTAs, one strong merchant/terminal visual.

2. **Trust band**
   Three verified proof points only: local support, processing coverage, transparent pricing.

3. **What Liberty handles**
   Processing, merchant accounts, terminal/POS setup, human support.

4. **Free analysis**
   Combine “what you receive,” the three-step process, and risk reversal into one section.

5. **Pricing options**
   Cash discount, compliant surcharging, interchange-plus. This can be the single major navy chapter.

6. **Setup by business type**
   Combine terminal setup and industries. Replace the administrative table with three visual setup cards.

7. **Verified merchant proof**
   One featured case study plus two smaller verified outcomes.

8. **FAQ**
   Keep the six strongest questions.

9. **Final CTA**
   Upload statement primary; book/callback secondary.

Remove from the homepage:

* Standalone pain-points section
* Standalone calculator
* Five-stage onboarding timeline
* Platform/dashboard preview
* Standalone “Why Liberty” rate table
* Standalone risk-reversal image section
* Processor-link row
* Full callback form

The calculator, detailed onboarding, comparisons, and callback form already have better dedicated-route homes.

## Hero replacement

Recommended copy:

* Badge: **South Florida Merchant Services**
* H1: **Credit Card Processing With Clear Pricing and Real Support**
* Subheadline: **Merchant accounts, modern terminals, transparent pricing, and a dedicated Liberty advisor—from your first review through go-live.**
* Primary CTA: **Get a Free Statement Review**
* Secondary CTA: **Book a 10-Minute Call**

Replace the audit-report graphic with either:

* A genuine merchant using a Liberty-supported terminal, or
* A clean terminal/POS product composition on a warm neutral background

Do not use ledger lines, dashboards, floating data cards, graphs, or generic meeting-room stock imagery in the hero.

## Mobile-specific repairs

The supplied screenshots are desktop-width, but the responsive code reveals additional mobile defects:

* Remove the `$600–$3,200/year` hero claim unless evidence is attached.
* Remove the duplicate hero phone row and duplicate trust rows.
* Change 2×2 dense text-card grids to single-column rows at 375px.
* Show a compact terminal/setup section on mobile; its current mobile markup is unreachable because the parent section is `hidden md:block`.
* Keep one sticky CTA only, 52–56px high.
* Ensure sticky CTA, chat widget, cookie consent, and safe-area padding never overlap.
* Use 24px page gutters at 430px and 16–20px at 375px.
* No horizontal clipping or `overflow-x-hidden` used to conceal layout errors.

## Visual direction

Use only four section treatments:

* White
* Warm ivory
* Soft slate
* Fixed deep navy

Reserve blue for links, focus states, and small trust accents. Use Liberty red sparingly for rules, active states, and brand moments. Remove bright-blue floating UI, grid backgrounds, gradients, and repeated thin divider lines.

## Release gates

Before approval, capture and review:

* Desktop: 1440×900 and 1920×1080
* Tablet: 768×1024 and 1024×1366
* Mobile: 375×812, 390×844, and 430×932
* OS dark and light preference
* JavaScript-enabled and SSR-only homepage

Required pass conditions:

* Zero WCAG AA contrast failures
* No text below 12px
* No horizontal overflow at 320px
* No sticky-widget collisions
* Homepage at or below ten major sections
* SSR and hydrated page have identical primary copy and claims
* All statistics, testimonials, savings ranges, and “all 50 states” statements have documented evidence or are removed
* No ledger, spreadsheet, dot, or grid texture on public marketing pages

The correct implementation order is: **theme/contrast repair → header → homepage reduction → hero replacement → mobile/sticky cleanup → SSR parity → visual regression QA**.
