AI Merchant Services Sales, Operations, Account Management & Profitability OS

Purpose: This document is the authoritative strategic build plan for Liberty Bancard.

Primary objective: Build Liberty Bancard into a closed-loop merchant-services operating system where Replit is the authoritative brain from lead acquisition through active processing, merchant retention, residual reconciliation, profitability, expansion, referrals, and win-back.
