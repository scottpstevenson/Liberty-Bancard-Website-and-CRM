It’s much cleaner than the earlier Liberty version. This is no longer broken-looking. The brand feels more legitimate, the CTAs are clear, and the mobile hero has a strong conversion path.

But critically: it’s still not 10/10. I’d call this 7.8/10.

What’s working

The logo/header feels professional now. It is clean, not cluttered, and the hamburger is simple.

The headline is strong. “Credit Card Processing for Businesses That Deserve Clearer Rates” is direct and benefit-driven. The blue serif treatment gives it some authority.

The CTA hierarchy is good. Upload statement first, book review second, call third. That is the right conversion order.

The supporting copy is believable. It explains merchant accounts, terminal setup, interchange-plus, Florida, and statement review without sounding spammy.

Biggest issues

1. There is way too much empty space before the hero starts

The header ends around the upper third of the screen, then there is a large blank gap before the little blue accent line and badge.

That makes the page feel like the hero is “floating too low.” On mobile, every pixel above the fold matters. You’re losing prime space before the actual pitch begins.

I would tighten the space between the nav and hero by 32–48px.

2. The small blue accent line feels random

The short horizontal blue line above the badge looks like a leftover decorative element. It does not connect to anything.

Either remove it or make it part of a more intentional system. Right now it feels like a random divider that accidentally survived a redesign.

3. The hero is a little too tall before proof appears

Above the fold includes:

* nav
* huge blank space
* badge
* H1
* paragraph
* two CTAs
* helper text
* call link

That’s a lot before the user sees proof, savings logic, review example, or trust. The conversion path is clear, but the page needs a little more “why trust this?” sooner.

A compact proof row under the CTAs would help:

Free statement review · No processor login needed · Florida-based · Interchange-plus guidance

4. The helper text under the CTAs is too long and too soft

Current:

PDF or photo. 30 seconds. Redact account numbers if you want — we only need totals + fee lines.

It’s useful, but visually it’s a bit washed out and line-breaks into too much space.

Better:

Upload a PDF or photo. Takes 30 seconds. You can redact account numbers — totals and fee lines are enough.

Make it slightly smaller or tighter, but a little darker.

5. The secondary CTA border is a little heavy

“Book a 15-Minute Review” looks solid, but the black/navy outline is very thick compared to the rest of the soft design. It makes the button feel slightly clunky.

Use a thinner border or softer navy opacity. Keep it clear, just less heavy.

6. H1 line breaks are okay but not perfect

The headline is readable, but the breaks are a bit chunky:

Credit Card Processing for
Businesses That Deserve
Clearer Rates

It works, but the first line is visually louder because “Credit Card Processing” is blue. Consider slightly reducing the H1 size or max-width so the rhythm feels more intentional.

7. The call link is too detached

“Call 954-266-8214” is helpful, but it sits alone with a lot of breathing room. It could be part of a compact contact row under the helper copy, or it could be visually grouped with the CTA area.

Right now it feels like a leftover line below the hero buttons.

My fixes in priority order

1. Reduce top hero whitespace after nav.
2. Remove the random blue accent line.
3. Tighten CTA/helper/call spacing.
4. Make helper text darker and cleaner.
5. Soften the secondary CTA border.
6. Add a compact proof/trust row near the CTAs.
7. Slightly tune H1 size/line-height on 375px.

Overall: much improved and credible, but it still needs mobile spacing polish. The current layout is clean, but too airy in the wrong places and a little under-supported above the fold.
