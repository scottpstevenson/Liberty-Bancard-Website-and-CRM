DEFINITION OF DONE

Wave 1 is complete only when:

1. Replit is authoritative over CRM/business state.
2. External utilities cannot independently control Liberty lifecycle.
3. Business code no longer relies on direct GHL workflow logic.
4. Outbound utilities are centrally orchestrated.
5. Compliance/contactability fences all outbound channels.
6. Provider events are normalized into Liberty.
7. GHL/provider outages do not corrupt Liberty state.
8. NBA exists for active entities.
9. NBA is auditable and value-aware.
10. NBA cannot bypass deterministic rules.
11. Existing lifecycle/automation/sequence systems remain working.
12. Pre-deploy tests pass.
13. Remaining GHL coupling is explicitly documented.
14. No duplicate replacement systems were created.

The core architectural test is:

If GHL workflows, opportunities, tasks and CRM pipeline disappeared tomorrow, Liberty’s business logic would still know exactly who every prospect/merchant is, what state they are in, and what should happen next. External channel delivery may be temporarily unavailable, but the Liberty operating system itself remains intact.
