789 — Automated Outreach Readiness Audit

What & Why

Liberty has built the discovery → A-Lead Queue → promote → Sales Prep → contactability → sequence enrollment → outreach infrastructure. The next question is whether we can safely turn on automated outreach.

This is a read-only audit to determine whether Liberty is ready for:

1. manual confirmed email-only outreach
2. semi-automated email outreach
3. fully automated email outreach
4. SMS / Voice AI / Ringless outreach

Do not change code in this task.

The purpose is to prove whether automated outreach can run without bypassing compliance, suppression, opt-outs, contactability, sequence safety, throttles, deliverability rules, or human review gates.

Final Verdict Required

Return one of these:

READY FOR FULL AUTO EMAIL OUTREACH
READY ONLY FOR SEMI-AUTO EMAIL OUTREACH
READY ONLY FOR MANUAL CONFIRMED EMAIL OUTREACH
NOT READY FOR OUTREACH

Also separately state:

SMS ready: yes/no
Voice AI ready: yes/no
Ringless voicemail ready: yes/no

Default assumption: SMS / Voice AI / Ringless are not ready unless explicitly proven through the existing channel approval gate and consent system.

⸻
