---
name: Existing email inventory diagnosis
description: Avoid mistaking a small discovery cohort's yield for the available email inventory.
---

For recipient-growth work, assess existing business, contact, and retained email-evidence inventory before recommending more paid discovery. Separate inventory size, intake coverage, cohort eligibility, validation, and send approval.

**Why:** The user challenged an explanation based on a tiny discovery batch when the project already held a large imported contact-email inventory. That explanation did not establish why existing addresses were absent from validation.

**How to apply:** Count the existing production inventory and trace its intake exclusions first. Do not call an address invalid merely because it has not entered the validation pipeline, and never bypass provenance, DBPR, suppression, or review safeguards to admit it.

Existing contact-to-business linking and downstream email qualification are core scope of the recipient-growth repair, not prerequisites to move into new follow-up tasks.

**Why:** The user repeatedly corrected explanations that merely reported missing business links even though producing those links was the requested work.

**How to apply:** Resume the approved linking/backfill execution and measure persisted verified relationships. Separate successful source tests, offline censuses, candidate reports, and published execution; none alone establishes completed linking.
