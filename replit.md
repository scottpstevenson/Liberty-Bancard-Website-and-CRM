# Liberty Bancard AI Business Operating System

## Overview
The Liberty Bancard AI Business Operating System is a comprehensive, AI-powered platform designed for the merchant payment processing industry. It integrates a public marketing website with advanced lead generation, an internal CRM for pipeline and task management, and an automated workflow engine with SLA enforcement. The system leverages AI for various functions, including specialized departmental advisors and compliance-driven communication. Its primary purpose is to streamline operations, enhance customer engagement, and boost sales efficiency, aiming to be a market leader in payment processing solutions.

## System Architecture
The system is built on a modern web stack, emphasizing scalability, responsiveness, and robust data management.

### UI/UX Decisions
- **Frontend**: React with Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and shadcn/ui for a responsive, modern user interface. `wouter` for client-side routing and `react-helmet-async` for SEO.

### Technical Implementations
- **Backend**: Express.js with TypeScript for API services.
- **Database**: PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM for data persistence.
- **Authentication**: Custom email/password authentication with session-based auth (passport-local + bcryptjs), including email verification, forgot password flow, and role-based access control. Admin account: scott@libertybancard.com.
- **AI Integration**: OpenAI powers department-specific AI advisors, lead enrichment, scoring, deal blueprint generation, and compliance-safe AI auto-replies for inbound messages.
- **External Communications**: GoHighLevel (GHL) is deeply integrated for SMS, email, calendar management, and document e-signature.

Includes SEO infrastructure (enhanced SEO component with JSON-LD, canonical URLs, keywords, OG/Twitter tags, noindex for auth pages), robots.txt, dynamic XML sitemap (143 URLs), 8 industry-specific landing pages (`/industries/:slug`), blog with 50 SEO articles (`/blog`, `/blog/:slug`), savings calculator (`/savings-calculator`), rate comparison tool (`/compare-rates`), Help Center/Knowledge Base (`/help`, `/help/:category/:slug`), AI-optimized FAQ (`/faq`, 31 Q&As with FAQPage schema), Why Liberty Bancard authority page (`/why-liberty-bancard`), Case Studies (`/case-studies`, 6 detailed savings stories), and 5 individual competitor comparison pages (`/compare/:competitor` — Square, Stripe, Clover, Toast, PayPal).

Full browse/detail/cart/checkout flow. Orders create CRM contact + deal + equipment_orders records with terminal assignment (`terminalRecommendation`, `terminalStatus`, `hardwarePackage` on deals). 24-hour mandatory setup & testing period before shipment. Public endpoint at `POST /api/equipment-order`.
- **Hidden Sales Enablement Pages** (noindex, no nav links): Equipment catalog (`/equipment`) with 6 terminals. Processing cost quiz (`/quiz/processing-cost`) with 6-step interactive flow and personalized recommendations. 7 industry/angle one-pagers (`/sales/:slug`): restaurant, retail, healthcare, home-services, ecommerce, cash-discount, switch. All shareable via direct URL.

Blog content split across `client/src/lib/blog-data.ts` through `blog-data-5.ts`, merged via `all-blog-data.ts`. Blog listing has category filtering, search, and pagination. Industry pages via `client/src/pages/IndustryPage.tsx`. Navbar with Industries/Tools dropdowns. Footer with comprehensive internal links across all sections.
- **Help Center / Knowledge Base**: 36 articles across 6 categories (Getting Started, Billing & Statements, Technical Support, Account Management, Compliance & Security, General FAQ). Data in `client/src/lib/help-center-data.ts`. Pages: `HelpCenter.tsx` (landing with search), `HelpArticle.tsx` (category listing + individual articles). Full SEO with breadcrumbs and FAQ structured data.
- **Legal & Compliance Pages**: 22 legal/consent pages including Privacy Policy, Terms, Cookie Policy, E-Sign Consent, SMS Terms, TCPA Consent, Surcharging Disclosure, Merchant Policies, Security & Compliance, Regulatory Notices, Data Processing Agreement, Data Retention, Responsible AI, Testimonials Disclosure, Law Enforcement Guidelines, Dispute Resolution, Do Not Sell, Advertising Disclosure, Accessibility, ADA Compliance, Refund Policy, and California Privacy (CCPA).
- **CRM Dashboard**: Modules for contact, sales pipeline (Kanban), support tickets, task management, notifications, and KPI digests.
- **AI Advisors**: Seven specialized AI advisors (Sales, Support, Onboarding, Marketing, Finance, Compliance, Executive) for guidance and automation.
- **Workflow Automation**: Centralized workflow execution engine (`server/services/workflow-executor.ts`) with 12+ action types and template interpolation. Triggered by various events (contact_created, deal_stage_changed, form_submitted, ticket_created, inbound_message) with SLA worker for waiting workflows.
- **Profit Instrumentation**: Integrates merchant tier, risk, health score, average ticket, and estimated profit into deals. Includes a volume estimation engine (`server/services/volume-estimator.ts`) for processing volume and residual calculations, with confidence levels improving through the sales cycle.
- **Lead Generation & Qualification**: Bulk CSV import, AI-powered prospect enrichment (website scraping, categorization, scoring), multi-step AI-personalized email campaigns, AI lead scoring (0-100 across 4 dimensions), AI deal blueprint generation, and document readiness tracking.
- **Lead Command Center**: Unified dashboard combining enrichment queue, qualified pipeline, and lead intelligence. Features mass actions, KPI cards, and detailed lead/company views. Includes proper case normalization for ingested data. Automated enrichment pipeline processes entities to prospects and promotes qualified prospects to contacts/deals.
- **Statement Review & Savings Proposals**: AI-powered analysis generating multi-plan pricing proposals (Cash Discount/Compliant Surcharging, Interchange Plus, Tiered Reduction) with savings calculations and compliance disclaimers.
- **Multi-Step Merchant Application**: Public 6-step wizard for merchant signup, including business, owner, bank, processing details, equipment, and program selection. E-signature handled via GHL Documents API.
- **Merchant Self-Service Portal**: Dashboard for merchants to view account status, onboarding progress, documents, and support tickets.
- **Compliance Rules**: Strict adherence to regulatory guidelines, including explicit disclaimers, no unsubstantiated savings claims, no legal/tax advice, and no PCI data storage. Features like TCPA Consent Audit Trail and PCI Compliance Self-Assessment.
- **Outreach Command Center** (`client/src/pages/dashboard/OutreachCommand.tsx`): Full pipeline dashboard for managing the automated sales lifecycle. Import FL Sunbiz entities (9.5GB corevt.zip), AI enrichment & classification, lead scoring (hot/warm/cold), promotion to CRM contacts + auto-deal creation, GHL 2-way sync, daily automated outreach (100/day), email signature management, worker status controls, and funnel visualization.
- **Full Corevt Import** (`server/services/daily-outreach.ts:importFullCorevt`): Streams 9.5GB Sunbiz corporate database ZIP, deduplicates by filing number, imports in batches of 200, tracks progress via system_settings.
- **Enhanced Enrichment Pipeline** (`server/services/sunbiz-enrichment.ts`): Zero guessing — discovers real websites via Google Search, scrapes actual business pages (homepage + /contact + /about), extracts contacts from Yelp business listings, scrapes Sunbiz detail pages for officer/agent data, AI classification via GPT-4o-mini with validated-only contact output (AI cannot fabricate emails/phones — must come from extracted sources). Sources tracked per entity in `enrichmentData.sources`.
- **2-Way GHL Sync** (`server/services/ghl-sync.ts`): Push contacts to GHL (`syncContactToGhl`), pull from GHL (`syncContactFromGhl`), bulk sync both directions (`fullSyncToGhl`, `fullSyncFromGhl`), deal sync (`syncDealToGhl`), matched by email/phone/ghlContactId.
- **Email Signatures** (`server/services/email-signatures.ts`): Sales, Support, Onboarding signatures with HTML and plain text. Editable via dashboard. Auto-injected into outbound emails.
- **Daily Outreach Automation** (`server/services/daily-outreach.ts`): Background worker runs enrichment every 30min + outreach pipeline every 60min. Cycle: enrich entities → promote qualified → auto-create deals → sync to GHL → queue campaign messages → send (100/day limit enforced end-to-end via budget passing) → auto-score/route/enroll in sequences. Email signatures auto-injected from stored/custom settings via `processSendQueue`.
- **Performance & Security**: All dashboard status endpoints use aggregate SQL queries (no full-table reads at 1M+ scale). GHL sync endpoints require admin/manager RBAC. Email signature HTML is XSS-sanitized via `escapeHtml`. Promoted counter only increments on actual contact/deal creation.
- **CRM Improvements**: Threaded comments, ticket conversation threading, contact/deal deduplication & merge, bulk stage updates, bulk task assignment, notification preferences, pipeline stage configuration, saved filters, soft delete/archive, contact-company many-to-many, advanced search.
- **Affiliate / Sales Team Program** (`/affiliate`): Public signup page for sales reps to join as affiliates, get unique referral codes/links, and track performance. `?ref=CODE` query param auto-tracked site-wide via localStorage, credited on all conversion forms (Get Started, Upload Statement, Estimate, Terminal Shop). Affiliate dashboard shows clicks, referrals, conversions, earnings. Admin CRM dashboard (`/dashboard/referral-program`) shows all affiliates with inline approve/suspend, commission rate editing, pending approval alerts, and affiliate code copying. Schema: `partners.affiliateCode` (unique), `partners.totalClicks`, `partners.paypalEmail`, `partners.website`, `partners.howHeard`. Public API: `POST /api/affiliate/signup`, `GET /api/affiliate/stats/:code`, `POST /api/affiliate/track-click`, `POST /api/affiliate/referral`.
- **Additional Modules**: Residual Revenue Dashboard, Agent/Rep Management, Merchant Health Monitoring, Competitive Win/Loss Tracking, Referral/Partner Program, Knowledge Base/FAQ, Review/Testimonial Requests, Onboarding Step Tracking, Equipment Order Management, Calendar/Appointment Booking, Revenue Forecasting, Comparative Analytics, Agent Quota/Goal Tracking, CSV Export, Bulk Messaging, Document Storage, Activity Timeline, Welcome Notifications, and Data Retention Policy.

## External Dependencies
- **PostgreSQL**: Primary database.
- **OpenAI API**: AI advisors, lead enrichment, scoring, deal blueprint generation, and AI auto-replies.
- **GoHighLevel (GHL) API**: Contact sync, email/SMS sending, calendar management, inbound webhooks, and e-signature.
- **Passport.js**: Authentication framework (passport-local + bcryptjs).
- **Multer**: Handles `multipart/form-data` for file uploads (CSV, corevt.zip).
